# PAYai – Revenue Model

## Overview

PAYai generates revenue across five distinct streams, ensuring a diversified and sustainable business model. The platform earns from both sides of each transaction (consumer interest and merchant fees) while also benefiting from blockchain infrastructure participation.

---

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Interest on Bridge Loans (Primary Revenue)

Consumers who use PAYai's bridge-credit facility are charged interest on the outstanding loan balance.

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Daily interest rate | 0.3 % – 0.8 % per day (risk-tiered) |
| Average loan amount | £35 |
| Average loan duration | 9 days |
| Example interest earned | £35 × 0.5 % × 9 days = £1.58 per loan |

Interest rates are set in compliance with regulatory caps (e.g. FCA HCSTC cap of 0.8 %/day and 100 % total cost cap in the UK).

**Revenue drivers:**
- Number of active consumers
- Average transaction value
- Loan utilisation rate (% of consumers who use the bridge credit)
- Average loan duration (shorter = more loans / month)

---

### 2. Merchant Service Fee

Merchants pay a per-transaction fee for the higher approval rates and faster settlement PAYai delivers.

| Merchant Tier | Fee Structure |
|---|---|
| Standard | 0.5 % of transaction value + £/$/€ 0.10 flat |
| Partner (supermarkets, volume) | Negotiated; typically 0.3 % + £0.07 flat |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing |

For transactions where PAYai uses its bridge-credit to approve a sale that would otherwise have been declined, an **approval uplift premium** of 0.2 % is charged on top of the standard fee.

---

### 3. Card Interchange Revenue

As a Mastercard / Visa card issuer, PAYai earns interchange income on every card transaction:

| Card Type | Interchange Rate (UK/EU) |
|---|---|
| Consumer debit | Up to 0.2 % of transaction value |
| Consumer credit | Up to 0.3 % of transaction value |
| Business / premium | Up to 1.5 % of transaction value |

Interchange is paid by the merchant's acquirer to PAYai (as the issuer) and forms a predictable base revenue stream.

---

### 4. XRP Ledger Validator Revenue

PAYai operates as a trusted validator on the XRP Ledger, enabling:

| Revenue Source | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Ripple ODL partnership | Revenue share on cross-border On-Demand Liquidity transactions routed through PAYai |
| Third-party fee savings | Avoided fees from external XRPL node providers (cost saving treated as equivalent revenue) |
| Strategic value | Validator status enables Ripple partnership negotiations, co-marketing, and network visibility |

---

### 5. Consumer Subscription (PAYai Plus)

An optional premium subscription for consumers who want enhanced features:

| Tier | Monthly Fee | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **PAYai Free** | £0 | Bridge loans up to £150, standard approval rate |
| **PAYai Plus** | £4.99 / month | Bridge loans up to £500, 0 % interest first 3 days, priority support |
| **PAYai Premium** | £9.99 / month | Bridge loans up to £2,000, 0 % interest first 7 days, travel insurance, cashback |

Subscription revenue provides predictable recurring income and reduces reliance on loan volume.

## Cost Structure

| Cost Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Cost of funds | Interest paid on capital borrowed to fund the loan float pool |
| Credit losses | Loans that are not repaid (default rate target: < 3 %) |
| Technology | Cloud infrastructure, XRPL node operations, BaaS partner fees |
| Regulatory | Compliance, legal, licensing costs |
| Customer acquisition | Marketing, referral programmes |
| Operations | Customer support, fraud operations |

---

## Path to Profitability

- **Break-even** targeted at ~200,000 active consumers (Year 2).
- Key lever: **default rate management** – every 1 % reduction in default rate adds ~£1M to EBITDA at Year 2 scale.
- **Float pool efficiency** – reducing the cost of capital for the loan float pool (e.g. via institutional investor partnerships or securitisation) significantly improves margins.
